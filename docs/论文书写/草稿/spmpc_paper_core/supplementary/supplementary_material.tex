\section{Execution Model, Dual Buffers, and Online Solver Freeze}

Execution identification starts at the timestamp of the final published command
$u^{\mathrm{pub}}$, not at the solver proposal. Linear and angular channels are
identified and validated separately. The implemented solver state is
\begin{equation}
X^{\mathrm{aug}}=
\operatorname{col}(p_x,p_y,\psi,v^r,s,\omega^r,z^\ell,b^v,b^\omega)
\in\mathbb R^{22},
\end{equation}
with five linear and seven angular pending-command entries. The 14-D execution
substate is $x^{\mathrm{exec}}=\operatorname{col}(v^r,\omega^r,b^v,b^\omega)$.
This source-level contract is implemented and development-tested; the physical
identification records and acceptance thresholds below remain \Pending{} until
the corresponding hardware gate is run.

\section{Gate Construction, Compatibility, and Data Separation}

The fitting tool evaluates the 9-D ellipsoid and 14-D execution box separately
and defines joint acceptance as their conjunction.  For the diagonal empirical
representation used by the target formulation,
\begin{equation}
  m_i(e)=\sum_r\left(\frac{e_r}{\rho_{r,i}}\right)^2,
  \qquad
  m_i(e)\leq1,
\end{equation}
with phase radii $\rho_{r,i}=\text{\Pending}$.  Online residual admission also
requires $e^{\mathrm{exec}}\in\mathcal B_i^{\mathrm{exec}}$ throughout the
horizon.  Held-out confusion metrics use the joint acceptance decision;
separate state-gate and execution-box admission rates expose which component
rejected a sample, so outside-box samples are neither discarded nor silently
conditioned away.

The frozen selection diagnostic is the false-accept rate among independently
labeled unrecovered samples,
\begin{equation}
  r_{\mathrm{FA}|\neg\mathrm{R}}
  =\frac{\mathrm{FA}}{\mathrm{FA}+\mathrm{TR}},
\end{equation}
whose Wilson upper bound is checked against the frozen limit.  Recovered-sample
coverage $\mathrm{TA}/(\mathrm{TA}+\mathrm{FR})$ is accompanied by a separate
Wilson interval.  The conditional false-safe diagnostic uses a different
denominator,
\begin{equation}
  r_{\mathrm{FS}|\mathrm{A}}
  =\frac{\mathrm{FA}}{\mathrm{TA}+\mathrm{FA}},
\end{equation}
which estimates $P(\mathrm{recovery\ fail}\mid\mathrm{gate\ accept})$ and is
reported with a Wilson interval computed from that accepted-sample denominator.
The preceding Wilson interval cannot be reused for this ratio.  It is
mathematically undefined when no sample is accepted; the tool then records a
false \texttt{defined} flag and null estimate/interval along with the zero
accepted count.  False rejection, compatibility-set
rejection, and the worst phase are reported alongside it.
