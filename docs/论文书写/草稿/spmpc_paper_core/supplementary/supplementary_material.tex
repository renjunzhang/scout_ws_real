\section{Command-Chain and Replay Audit}

The command-chain archive covers all Stage~I conditions. For online \MPCC{}
conditions, the raw layer is the solver output; for Fixed-profile, it is the
method-native tracker command. Every record includes post-gate and published
commands, terminal/gate/limiter/fallback flags, tracker or solver status, and
the previous published command. The report stratifies $r_{\mathrm{int}}$,
raw-to-published differences, tracking error, latency, and failures by condition.

The RQ3 replay clones online-input and zero-state branches from the same
immutable pre-solve snapshot. The online-input branch must reproduce solver
status, first action, and raw command within frozen tolerances:

Each \SMPCC{} trial yields trial-level first-action/timing summaries and the
fraction of cycles above tolerance. Control cycles are not independent samples.
Unless both branches pass through identical execution-layer snapshots, the
reported quantities remain optimized differences, not counterfactual executed
commands. The online-input state is not described as a measured physical state.

\section{Development-Only Provenance}

Historical physical data, candidate-weight trials, rotation studies, comparator
tuning, and efficacy pilots are development-only. Each record contains date,
path/container, software/configuration hash, candidate label, tuning budget,
randomization, measurement version, and its role in selection or validation.
No development trial contributes to formal $n$.

Historical batches have not yielded a stable ranking. In one same-day
development reanalysis, the then-current \texttt{B\_slosh} release was worse
than \texttt{B\_smooth} in RGB p95, peak, and RMS by approximately
$32.5\%$, $26.4\%$, and $21.4\%$, respectively, despite the opposite internal
model ranking. Older three-trial summaries with favorable values correspond to
different, incompletely frozen provenance and are retained only in the
repository history. This inconsistency is the reason the independent RGB
efficacy gate cannot be replaced by $H_{\mathrm{modal}}$ screening.

\section{Sensitivity, Runtime, and K6-Style Diagnostics}

Supporting simulation or replay perturbations include $\omega_1$, $\zeta$,
$c_h$, constructed internal modal state/phase, execution delay, and, if $C_2$
is retained, correct versus incorrect container parameters. Parameter mismatch
in replay establishes optimizer sensitivity only.

Runtime tables cover all five Stage~I conditions, using solver cost for online
\MPCC{} and separate offline-generation/online-tracking cost for Fixed-profile.
They report median, p95, maximum, budget-overrun rate, method failures,
fallbacks, intervention, and achieved frequency. Command total variation,
finite-difference acceleration, direction reversal, and spectral energy are
supporting diagnostics rather than primary trajectory outcomes.

The K6-style modal--vision audit uses only planned \SMPCC{} units: 8 after
Stage~I, 16 after Stage~II-A, and 24 after conditional Stage~II-B. A single
analysis script, synchronization/calibration record, lag policy, failure
denominator, and no per-trial best fit are frozen. The diagnostic compares
nonnegative amplitude envelopes; it does not validate signed phase, true
liquid-state observation, or rotation consistency. Visual measurement admission
remains mandatory even if the full model-consistency analysis is not promoted to
the main paper.

\section{Optional Exploratory Diagnostics}

Long-horizon propagation, physical parameter mismatch, and additional planner
comparisons have no reserved sample size and do not change the 40/64/88 matrix.
They are added only under a separate preregistered exploratory protocol after
the main evidence chain is complete. Unexecuted exploratory studies do not
appear as pending sections in the submission manuscript.
